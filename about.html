ABOUT ME

Two disciplines, one habit of trying

I'm Ibrahim Aliyu Aliyu, a final-year Computer Engineering undergraduate at Ahmadu Bello University (ABU), Zaria. My technical grounding started with a Diploma in Computer System Engineering in 2021, and I've spent the years since building on it through networking coursework, hardware diagnostics, and — more recently — exploring what generative AI tools can do for engineering and business work alike.

Outside the classroom, I run Maigoro Enterprises, a clothing line and custom fashion accessories brand I founded. It started as a side interest and has become a genuine second discipline: sourcing, tailoring concepts, client relationships, and brand-building all sit alongside my engineering coursework.

I don't see the two as a contradiction. Engineering has taught me to be precise; running a business has taught me to be decisive with incomplete information. Both need the same starting move — trying.

"If you never try, you will never know."
